## 3. Understanding the Difference Between NumPy and Pandas

### NumPy
- Mainly focuses on numerical calculations and arrays.
- Provides tools for mathematical and statistical operations.
- Efficiently handles numerical data.

### Pandas
- Mainly focuses on structured data and tables.
- Provides tools for organising and manipulating datasets.
- Makes it easier to explore and analyse information.
